02 / SYNITH

The Original SYN

The story begins with SYNITH, the original electronic music identity.

SYNITH wasn't just a name chosen because it sounded electronic.

The name came from a word that was created specifically around the feeling produced by synthesizers. It was inspired by frisson, the physical and emotional reaction people can experience when music hits particularly hard.

SYNITH was essentially your own concept for that feeling:

The emotional and physical sensation created by synthesizers and electronic music.

SYNITH was primarily rooted in:

Dubstep • Riddim • EDM • Electronic Music

During this period, SYNITH developed connections within the electronic music scene, including work with Irregular Fantasies and a period of involvement with xKILLAx.

05 / THE SYNITH PROBLEM

When Search Couldn't Find SYNITH

There was one major problem with the name SYNITH:

Search engines didn't necessarily understand that SYNITH was an artist.

Searching for SYNITH often produced synthesizer-related results instead of the actual artist and music.

The name had history, but it was difficult to establish a unique searchable identity.

That problem would eventually influence the branding strategy behind LIL SYNN.

06 / SYNTIENT

The SYN Identity Evolves

The SYN concept eventually evolved into SYNTIENT.

The name retained the central SYN identity while expanding beyond the original SYNITH concept.

SYNTIENT eventually became more than an artist identity. It became the foundation of the label.

08 / LIL SYN

The Lyrical Identity Is Born

Eventually, the music needed an identity that could represent something different from the SYNSTATIC project.

A new persona was needed for:

  • Lyrics
  • Songwriting
  • Relationships
  • Emotion
  • Storytelling
  • Pop
  • Cinematic music

That identity became:

LIL SYN

LIL SYN was not a random rebrand. It was a natural extension of the existing SYN identity into a more lyrical and character-driven form.

But there was a problem.

The name was already taken.

09 / LIL SYNN

The Final Name

LIL SYN eventually became:

LIL SYNN

10 / THE LIL SYNN PERSONA

The Faceless Character

LIL SYNN eventually became more than an artist name.

The character developed into a mysterious, faceless persona.

Signature appearance

  • Black long-sleeve hoodie with the hood up
  • Skin-tight face covering with the eyes concealed
  • Dark blue jeans
  • Black combat boots
  • Black leather gloves
  • Black and silver belt
  • Pink sunglasses
  • Small gold cross necklace

The faceless presentation makes LIL SYNN feel like a character rather than simply a photograph of the person behind the music.

The visual identity reflects the music:

Mysterious.
Emotional.
Dark.
Cinematic.
Vulnerable without being visually exposed.

11 / THE MUSIC EVOLVES

Cinematic Emotrap + Pop

Cinematic Emotrap + Pop

  • Vulnerability
