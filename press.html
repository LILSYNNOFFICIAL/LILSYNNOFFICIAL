THE ARTIST.

A concise industry-facing overview of the LIL SYNN project.

LIL SYNN is a faceless artist persona where music, visual art, technology and storytelling intersect. The project uses artificial intelligence as part of a broader creative workflow while keeping the artist identity, emotional direction and world-building at the center.

The result is a deliberately unconventional music project built around dark electronic pop, raw emotion, visual identity and an evolving digital universe.
