SUNO STUDIO 2.0

A practical current guide to Suno's desktop production environment: timeline editing, Take Lanes, Chat Bar, stems, MIDI, synths, effects, automation and export.

BACK TO SUNO HOMECONTROL HUB

WHAT STUDIO 2.0 IS

Studio 2.0 is Suno's full production environment for shaping generated material. It combines generative editing with a DAW-like timeline, audio effects, automation, MIDI, a built-in wavetable synth, custom AI-generated plugins and stem workflows.

Availability: Premier. Suno recommends current Google Chrome. Studio requires a desktop, laptop or tablet with at least 768px screen width and is not supported on mobile devices.

OPENING A SONG

  1. Open your Library or Workspace.
  2. Open the song's More Actions menu.
  3. Choose Edit, then Open in Studio.
  4. Preserve the original before making destructive-looking changes.

THE TIMELINE

Studio uses tracks and clips to arrange audio and MIDI. Transport controls sit above the timeline in 2.0. Play, stop, metronome, loop, follow-playhead, tempo, clock and time-signature controls are available from the top area.

Take Lanes

Every generation creates two takes. Take Lanes let you audition alternatives, rate them and promote the preferred clips onto the main track. Replaced material remains recoverable, making Take Lanes the safest place to compare generated alternatives.

CHAT BAR

The Chat Bar is the generative control surface. Describe the change you want in plain language. Depending on the request, Studio can generate audio or MIDI, alter arrangement material, create effects, design plugins or answer project questions.

GOOD: "Replace the bridge with a darker synth-only breakdown and keep the vocal timing."

Be specific about what must stay unchanged. Treat the result as a new take that needs auditioning, not as a guaranteed exact edit.

STEMS

Right-click an audio clip and choose Split Stems. Current Studio supports the newer stem separation modes. Extracted parts appear as timing-aligned tracks. Mute the source track if you want the extracted stems to replace it.

Stem separation is not perfect. Check vocals, drums, bass, cymbals, ambience and stereo artifacts before building a final mix around an extraction.

MIDI

MIDI tracks contain notes rather than recorded audio. Studio provides a piano roll for drawing and editing notes, musical typing, external MIDI input, quantization, velocity and pitch/modulation controls.

You can use the built-in instruments to audition MIDI immediately. Audio can also be transcribed into MIDI, after which the notes can be edited before generating or playing the resulting part.

WAVETABLE SYNTH

Studio includes a real-time wavetable synth with oscillators, filters, envelopes, LFOs and modulation controls. MIDI tracks can drive it directly, making it useful when you want note-level control over a generated arrangement.

EFFECTS

Built-in effects include Compressor, Convolution, Delay, Distortion, EQ, Gate and Reverb. Effects can be automated and controlled through MIDI Learn when supported.

Important: Studio is not a conventional VST or Audio Units host. Its custom plugins are Studio-native plugins generated through the Chat Bar.

AUTOMATION

Automation lets you draw changes over time for track controls such as volume, pan and plugin parameters. Use it for transitions, builds, drops, vocal rides, effect throws and movement that should happen at exact timeline positions.

IMPORT + EXPORT

  • Import: WAV, MP3 and MIDI.
  • Export: full song, selected range or multitrack project output.
  • Individual stems can be downloaded as WAV.
  • Studio does not provide direct DAW synchronization.

RECOMMENDED WORKFLOW

  1. Generate: make the strongest musical version you can.
  2. Diagnose: identify the exact bad section or production problem.
  3. Preserve: keep the original and use Take Lanes for alternatives.
  4. Repair: use Replace/Edit for local problems instead of regenerating the whole song.
  5. Separate: use stems only when independent processing is worth the extraction tradeoff.
  6. Engineer: use MIDI, synths, effects and automation for detailed control.
  7. QC: check timing, artifacts, mono compatibility, clipping and transitions.
  8. Export: render the approved full mix, range or multitrack.

COMMON MISTAKES

  • Regenerating the entire song when one section is wrong.
  • Assuming a generated take is automatically better because it is newer.
  • Building a final mix around a stem without auditioning artifacts.
  • Ignoring timing drift in generated clips.
  • Expecting VST/AU plugins to load in Studio.
  • Using Studio on an unsupported mobile layout.

CURRENT STATUS
