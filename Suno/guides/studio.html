Studio 2.0

Suno Studio 2.0 turns the generative workflow into a production environment with automation, effects, MIDI, synths and conversational control.

PRODUCESTUDIOPREMIER

What changed

  • Chat Bar for conversational production actions.
  • Automation for volume, panning and plugin parameters.
  • Real-time effects and custom plugin creation.
  • MIDI tracks, piano roll, musical typing and external MIDI input.
  • Wavetable synthesis and advanced stem separation.

Production mindset

Once the composition is strong, move downstream: edit clips, shape arrangement, process tracks, automate movement, verify timing and export the final material.
