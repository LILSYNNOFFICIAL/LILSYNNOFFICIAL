MIDI tools

Studio 2.0 includes MIDI tracks, a piano roll, musical typing, external MIDI input and audio-to-MIDI transcription. MIDI stores notes rather than recorded audio, making later note edits possible.

Wavetable

The built-in Wavetable synth provides playable real-time instruments with oscillators, filters, envelopes, LFOs and modulation controls.
