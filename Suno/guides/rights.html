Rights & Release

Treat plan-dependent rights, downloads and release requirements as current documentation questions, not assumptions from old tutorials.

PRODUCERIGHTSRELEASE

Release checklist

  • Confirm the plan and applicable commercial-rights terms for the creation.
  • Confirm lyrics, samples and uploaded source material are yours to use.
  • Export the correct format and preserve stems/project assets as needed.
  • Complete metadata, artwork, lyrics and platform delivery.

Evidence rule

Rights and download policies can change. Always use Suno's current official policy pages for the final decision on a release.
