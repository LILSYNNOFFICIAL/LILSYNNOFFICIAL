Rights & Release

Treat commercial-use rights, downloads, ownership and release requirements as current policy questions—not assumptions from old tutorials.

PRODUCERIGHTSRELEASE

Current commercial-use rule

Suno's current policy says songs downloaded while subscribed to a paid plan receive commercial-use rights. Those rights allow monetization, but Suno explicitly does not guarantee copyright protection.

Current download allowances

  • Free: up to 7 lifetime trial downloads under the current trial policy; trial downloads are personal/non-commercial.
  • Pro: 20 song downloads per month.
  • Premier: 60 song downloads per month.
  • Pro/Premier allowances reset on the billing date and unused downloads do not roll over.
  • Downloading the same song again or downloading multiple formats of the same song does not consume another song download; stems are included with that song's download.
  • Studio workflows are not subject to the standard download limits.

Copyright is a separate question

Commercial-use permission from Suno is not the same thing as copyright eligibility. Copyright rules vary by jurisdiction and can depend on human authorship and the underlying inputs. If you wrote lyrics, retain the original lyric records. Do not assume a prompt alone creates copyright in an AI-generated musical output.

Release checklist

  • Confirm the plan and the download status that applies to the final file.
  • Confirm lyrics, samples, voices and uploaded source material are yours to use or properly authorized.
  • Preserve final WAV/MP3, stems, MIDI and project assets where appropriate.
  • Finalize metadata, artwork, lyrics and delivery requirements.
  • Keep a release record containing plan status, download status and source/permission notes.

Evidence rule

Rights and download policies can change quickly. Use the linked official Suno policy pages for the final release decision.
