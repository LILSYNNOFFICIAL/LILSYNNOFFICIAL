Stem-first thinking

Stem separation lets you isolate musical elements so you can rebalance, replace or process individual parts. Use stems when the composition is right but the mix relationship is not.

Remaster

Remaster is suited to controlled variations of a finished clip. Compare the result against the original rather than assuming “newer” means better.

Rescue rule

GREAT SONG + BAD MIX → STEMS / STUDIO
GREAT SONG + SMALL SONIC ISSUE → REMASTER / LOCAL EDIT
BAD SONG → RETURN TO CREATIVE LAYER
