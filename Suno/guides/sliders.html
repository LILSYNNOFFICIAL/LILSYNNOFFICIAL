Creative Controls

Treat sliders and Advanced Options as variables in a controlled experiment, not magic quality knobs.

CONTROLSLIDERSTESTING

Variety

Suno describes Variety as a control that introduces variety by adjusting and updating style prompts. Lower it when you want tighter control of your style tags.

Max Mode

The current V6 FAQ says Max Mode costs more credits and is best suited to longer songs, close covers, style transfer and consistency. Use standard generation for quick ideas and shorter songs.

Test one variable

Keep lyrics, model and core style fixed while comparing one control. Save the winning variant before changing the next variable.
