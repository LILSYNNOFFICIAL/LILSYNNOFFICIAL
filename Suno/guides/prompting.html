Prompt Engineering

Translate intent into prioritized, testable instructions without drowning the model in adjective soup.

CONTROLPROMPTSV6

Prompt hierarchy

When shortening a prompt, protect the information that defines the song first: primary identity, vocal identity, core instruments, groove, arrangement, dynamics, atmosphere, mix and master character.

PRIMARY IDENTITY
+ VOCAL
+ CORE INSTRUMENTS
+ GROOVE
+ ARRANGEMENT
+ DYNAMICS
+ ATMOSPHERE
+ MIX
+ MASTER

Style is behavior

Prefer audible descriptions such as “dry intimate vocal,” “controlled low end,” “wide chorus,” and “defined transients” over stacks of vague quality adjectives.

Negative prompting

Use exclusions when a recurring failure needs correction. Keep them specific and local rather than filling the prompt with dozens of prohibitions.
