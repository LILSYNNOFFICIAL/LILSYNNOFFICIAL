Local direction

Place performance or arrangement guidance near the section it affects. Keep song-wide sound in Style rather than repeating production instructions on every lyric line.

Chorus design

Prioritize a recognizable hook, repeatable phrasing, melodic room for emphasis and an emotional lift that feels larger than the verse.
