Surgical Editing

When the song is good except for one thing, fix the smallest possible region instead of throwing away the entire performance.

CONTROLEDITREPAIR

Choose the smallest tool

  • Extend: continue the song or create a new ending.
  • Crop: remove unwanted material from the edges.
  • Replace Section: rebuild a local section while preserving the rest.
  • Remix / Reuse Prompt: explore a controlled variation.

Repair principle

Identify whether the problem is lyric, vocal, arrangement, timing, sound selection or mix. Use the tool that owns that layer.
