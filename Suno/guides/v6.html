V6 Fundamentals

Choose the right V6-family model for the job, then treat generation as a controlled creative experiment.

CREATEMODELSCURRENT

The current family

Suno currently describes v6 as its flagship model, v6-wild as its experimental variant, and v6-mini as the faster version available to all users.

  • v6: precise, expressive and the default choice when you know what you want.
  • v6-wild: less predictable and useful for discovery.
  • v6-mini: faster iteration and concept testing.

Model-switching strategy

Use mini to explore, wild to discover unexpected directions, and v6 to refine a defined target. Keep the creative variable you are testing isolated so you can tell whether the model actually improved the result.

CONCEPT → V6-MINI → DISCOVERY / V6-WILD → REFINEMENT / V6 → EDIT → STUDIO

Control principle

Do not regenerate a strong song merely to solve a local defect. Preserve the good information and use the smallest layer capable of repairing the problem.
