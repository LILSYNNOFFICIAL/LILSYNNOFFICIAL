V6 Fundamentals

Choose the right V6-family model for the job, then treat generation as a controlled creative experiment.

CREATEMODELSCURRENT · SEPTEMBER 2026

The current family

Suno's current V6 family has three variants:

  • v6: the flagship model for precise, expressive, polished generation; available to Pro and Premier.
  • v6-wild: the less predictable experimental variant for discovery; available to Pro and Premier.
  • v6-mini: the faster V6-family variant available to all users.

All three support up to eight minutes per generation.

Model-switching strategy

Use mini for rapid concept exploration, wild when you want unexpected directions, and v6 when you have a defined target that needs controlled refinement. Keep the creative variable you are testing isolated so you can tell whether the model actually improved the result.

CONCEPT → V6-MINI → DISCOVERY / V6-WILD → REFINEMENT / V6 → EDIT → STUDIO

Max Mode

Max Mode is a current V6 option for spending more on a generation when fidelity matters. Suno recommends it especially for songs longer than two minutes, close covers, style transfer and consistency-sensitive vocals/style. Standard mode is generally appropriate for quick ideas and shorter songs.

Generation length

V6, v6-wild and v6-mini can generate up to eight minutes in a single generation. Extend remains available when you need to continue beyond the generated section.

Control principle

Do not regenerate a strong song merely to solve a local defect. Preserve the good information and use the smallest layer capable of repairing the problem.
