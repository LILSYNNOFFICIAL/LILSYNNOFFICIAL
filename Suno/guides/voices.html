Voices

Suno's Voices workflow lets creators use a verified voice profile in compatible generations. Suno requires a voice recording and a verification step designed to confirm that the uploaded/recorded voice belongs to the person creating the profile. Voices availability can vary by account and region and is restricted to users 18 and older.

Current Suno documentation says a voice source can be recorded or uploaded, with supported source material ranging from 15 seconds to 4 minutes; the creator can choose the best two minutes for the profile. Clean acapella material generally gives the strongest starting point.

Custom Models

Official documentation says Custom Models are available to Pro and Premier users. You can build a model from at least six songs, and you must have the rights to the songs you upload. Custom Models are private and are intended for personalized sound consistency.

Compatibility rule

Do not carry old Voice recipes forward blindly. V6 is the current generation family, and Suno's live compatibility guidance should take precedence over older Voice FAQ wording that references retired models.
