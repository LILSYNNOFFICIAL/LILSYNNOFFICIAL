EXPERIMENTAL

Experimental Suno behavior can sometimes be explored by deliberately entering unusual symbols or random phrases into the Lyrics and Styles boxes. These techniques are not documented controls and can produce different results from generation to generation, so treat them as experimentation rather than guaranteed commands.

SYMBOL EXPERIMENTATION

Symbols can yield various results when entered into the Lyrics and Styles boxes. Try unusual combinations, repetitions, sequences, and mixes of symbols to explore unexpected behaviors and textures.

These symbols can be found at EmojiCombos.com.

Example song using this type of symbol experimentation: https://suno.com/song/b073c28f-b338-4e07-a1fb-25aeb6342246

RANDOM PHRASE EXPERIMENTATION

Another experimental technique is entering random, invented, or unconventional phrases into the Styles box. The phrase does not need to describe a conventional genre or production instruction; unusual strings may sometimes influence the resulting generation in interesting ways.

Example phrases include glitch_matrix.exe or umbrella_milkshake2.dll.

Example song demonstrating this type of Styles-box experimentation: https://suno.com/song/26937bed-2eae-47cd-92f4-63aa2bf07df0

EXPERIMENTAL NOTE

There is no guarantee that a particular symbol, sequence, or random phrase will produce the same effect every time. Record the exact input, model, settings, and generation when testing so useful patterns can be reproduced and compared.
