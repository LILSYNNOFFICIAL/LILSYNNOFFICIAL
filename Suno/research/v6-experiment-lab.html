PHASE 12 / EVIDENCE GENERATION

V6 EXPERIMENT LAB

A controlled research bench for Suno V6. The goal is not to collect prompt superstition. The goal is to isolate variables, generate multiple samples, record failures, repeat the test and promote only reproducible findings.

BACK TO SUNO GUIDEOPEN EVIDENCE LEDGER
Current baseline: Suno identifies v6 as its flagship model, v6-wild as the more experimental branch, and v6-mini as the faster v6 option. These are documented product facts; the experiments below test behavior beyond those official descriptions. Official documentation must outrank community claims and one-off results.

06 — MODEL TRIAD

Use the same creative brief on V6, V6-WILD and V6-MINI. Score adherence, vocals, arrangement, dynamics, clarity, novelty and failures.

07 — AUDIO REFERENCE

Compare text-only creation against the same text plus a reference. Score timing, articulation, timbral relationships, arrangement feel and performance texture.

08 — LOCAL REPAIR

When one section fails, compare full regeneration against Edit/Replace-style local repair and measure what useful material survives.

09 — REMASTER BOUNDARY

Test dullness, masking, reverb, low-end weakness and unwanted arrangement changes to identify what Remaster can and cannot reliably rescue.

10 — CHORUS REPEATABILITY

Hold Style fixed while changing one lyric-geometry variable: syllable density, line length, punctuation, wording, breaks or cue.

LYRICSTARGET C

11 — PROMPT DENSITY

Compare identity-only, identity+instrumentation, identity+performance+arrangement, and full producer brief. Find the point where detail stops helping.

FAILURE CORPUS

Log failures as first-class evidence: buried/distant vocal, unexpected register, timbre drift, chorus inconsistency, unwanted doubles/choir, instrument disappearance, unexpected instrumentation, genre drift, stuttering/repeated fragments, late-song degradation, volume drift, excessive reverb, harshness/hiss, low-end buildup, clipping/artifacts, phase/stereo instability, premature ending, vocal continuation past the musical ending, and ignored section direction.

RESEARCH RULE

A better-sounding generation is not automatically proof of causation. A worse generation is not proof that a technique never works. A finding becomes strong evidence only when the variable is isolated, multiple generations agree, the effect survives repetition, conditions are preserved, and plausible competing explanations have been tested.
