# PHASE 10 — /Suno CONTENT AUDIT

Date: 2026-09-15

## Objective

Audit the guide as a connected knowledge system, not as a pile of newly added pages. The goal is to make research findings land in the section where a user actually needs them while preserving the evidence boundary between official behavior, repeated community observation, direct experiments and hypotheses.

## Content map

### CREATE

The Create section now acts as the front door for:

- current V6 model selection
- prompting and Style-box architecture
- lyric engineering
- structure and meta-tag experimentation
- creative sliders and advanced options
- references and multimodal creation
- vocal engineering
- the Prompt / Tag Lab

Phase 4 lyric-control research remains embedded directly in Create so lyric geometry, punctuation, whitespace, phonetics, performance cues and chorus construction are not stranded in Research.

Phase 5 vocal engineering is linked directly from Create because vocal identity, register, tessitura, delivery and harmony decisions belong at creation time.

## Cross-reference rules now in force

1. **Creation knowledge belongs in Create.** Research can explain the evidence, but a creator should not have to leave Create to learn the practical technique.
2. **Editing knowledge belongs in Control.** Replace, Extend, Remaster, Stems and Studio behavior should be explained where the tool is used.
3. **Production knowledge belongs in Produce.** Mixing, arrangement, vocal engineering and export concepts should be reachable from the production workflow.
4. **Failure analysis belongs in Fix / Test.** A research observation becomes useful when it can be turned into a diagnostic test or repair decision.
5. **Experimental findings belong in Research.** Unverified syntax, symbols, gibberish and formatting effects stay labeled experimental instead of becoming fake API documentation.
6. **Canonical synthesis belongs in Master.** The Master Guide should collect the source corpus without replacing the specialized workflow pages.

## Evidence policy

Every new technique should be described with one of these boundaries:

- **A — Official:** directly documented by Suno.
- **B — Repeated:** repeated independent community observation.
- **C — Reproducible:** directly reproduced under recorded conditions.
- **D — Hypothesis:** plausible explanation not yet sufficiently tested.
- **E — Experimental:** intentionally weird or insufficiently established behavior.

No undocumented bracket tag, symbol, invented filename, punctuation pattern or slider setting is to be described as a guaranteed command.

## Coverage gaps intentionally left open

Some behaviors cannot be responsibly upgraded to “confirmed” without fresh controlled generations. These remain research questions:

- exact semantics of undocumented bracket tags
- fixed meanings for arbitrary Unicode symbols
- deterministic effects of gibberish or fake filenames
- universal slider recipes
- guaranteed hierarchy when prompts contain contradictions
- universal chorus-repeatability fixes
- universal audio-quality rescue prompts

The guide documents how to test these questions rather than pretending the evidence is stronger than it is.

## Phase 10 result

The V6 overhaul is now distributed across the guide architecture instead of being isolated in one oversized Research page. Research provides the evidence and experiments; Create, Control, Produce and Fix / Test provide the operational context; Master provides the canonical synthesis.
