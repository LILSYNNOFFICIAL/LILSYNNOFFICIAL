THE ARTIST

LIL SYNN

LIL SYNN is a Fresno, California artist building a dark, atmospheric world through music. The sound moves between electronic textures, melodic trap, ambient R&B and emotionally driven songwriting.

Music has been a lifelong goal — a way to tell stories, connect with listeners and turn difficult experiences into something people can feel.
