## The story

The adventure begins in a beautiful enchanted meadow, where **LIL SYNN** lives peacefully alongside a mysterious **girl in a yellow dress** and his fluffy black **cat** companion. But beneath the beauty of this magical world lies an ancient power waiting to be discovered.

LIL SYNN soon learns that music is more than sound: it is magic. Through his enchanted **microphone** he can absorb the hearts left behind by defeated enemies and transform them into **pink magical energy** that fuels his musical attacks.

Everything changes when a mysterious figure known as **Shadow Man** appears and takes the girl away through an unstable magical portal. Confused and determined, LIL SYNN sets out after her with his loyal cat.

At first LIL SYNN believes Shadow Man is his enemy. But things are not what they seem. **Shadow Man isn't evil. He is a mysterious guide** who has set a series of challenges in motion to teach LIL SYNN some of life's most important lessons: how to be brave when he's afraid, strong when life becomes difficult, kind when others need help, and compassionate even when he doesn't understand someone else's struggles.

The journey crosses enchanted forests, ancient kingdoms, underwater civilizations, floating islands, corrupted cities, forgotten realms and the far reaches of outer space. Ancient inscriptions reveal fragments of power that affect whole worlds, a spreading corruption, and a much larger mystery involving the origins of musical magic and a magnificent **Pink Dragon**. Along the way LIL SYNN helps the cat find its missing kittens, recovers his own lost microphone, and meets the gentle **Dreaming Whale**, whose glowing world connects dimensions.

In the **Dragon's Realm**, LIL SYNN faces his greatest challenge. Victory depends not on strength alone but on everything Shadow Man has taught him: courage, compassion, kindness, and the ability to understand rather than simply fight. Finally reunited with the girl, the adventure becomes a concert: the lights rise and the real recording of **"Rescue You (Acoustic)"** begins, while Shadow Man watches from a distance, his purpose understood.

> *True strength isn't about how powerful you are, but about who you choose to be.*

## Gameplay

### Controls

| Action | Desktop | Mobile / touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | `W` `A` `S` `D` / arrow keys | Left virtual joystick |
| Run / sprint | Hold `Shift` (keep running to sprint) | Push the stick all the way |
| Jump / double jump | `Space` (again in the air) | **JUMP** |
| Camera | Mouse (click the game to lock the mouse) or drag | Drag on the right side of the screen |
| Aim | Mouse: a small crosshair appears only while the mouse moves | Soft auto-aim at the nearest target in front |
| Sonic Blast / fire weapon | `E` or click | **♪** |
| Switch weapon | `Q`, mouse wheel, `1` `2` `3` | **⇄** |
| Microphone: absorb hearts into mana | `R` | **MIC** |
| Interact (chest, cat, the girl) | `F` | **USE** |
| Use a health pack | `H` | **✚** |
| Pause | `Esc` / `P` | **II** |
| Music on/off | `M` | Settings |
| Secret codes | `Ctrl` **on the main menu only** | **🔑 SECRET CODE** button (or long-press the title) on the main menu |

The game detects the input you are using and switches the on-screen hints and touch buttons automatically. On phones it fills the screen in portrait or landscape, respects notches and safe areas, and never scrolls or zooms.

### Mechanics

- **Hearts → microphone → mana.** Purified enemies drop pink hearts. Collect them, then raise the microphone: hearts spiral into it with notes and light, and every heart becomes +20 pink mana.
- **Sonic Blast.** LIL SYNN's first magic fires pink musical notes (2 mana) with trails, impact rings and synthesized musical sound. More weapons arrive as you explore: the **Star Wand** (triple starburst, 8 mana) and the secret **Pink Magic Cello** (an area wave dealing exactly 75% damage to every enemy it reaches).
- **Health hearts** crack and break as you take hits; **health packs** are collectible and usable.
- **Gloomlings** are gentle corrupted creatures, not monsters. Music washes the gloom away, they burst into sparkles, and a flower blooms where they stood.
- **The cat** follows you, sits, grooms, plays, naps, warns you about gloom, and has its own interaction menu. Its **Lost Kittens** quest begins in Level 1.
- **Secret codes** work only from the main menu (never in gameplay or the pause menu), are case-insensitive, never touch your progress, and are saved: `ROSIE`, `LIL SYNN`, `YELLOW DRESS`, `SHADOW MAN`, `CELLO`, `PINK`, `JARRED` and more.
- **The 369-level magic sword** grows through normal play (codes can unlock Level 5 or Level 369).
- **The Dreaming Whale**, a gigantic pink whale with its own world inside, is foreshadowed from the very first meadow.
- **Saves**: autosave after every lesson, three manual slots, versioned with migrations and verified writes, with automatic recovery from a corrupted save.
- **Audio**: LIL SYNN's own music plays at random (no back-to-back repeats, exploration vs. combat moods), and music and sound effects have independent on/off and volume controls in both the main menu and the pause menu.

## Tech

- **Engine:** hand-written JavaScript on **Three.js r160** (vendored in `vendor/three/`, MIT). No build step, no server dependencies: a self-contained static site.
- **Rendering:** PBR materials, HDRI image-based lighting (Poly Haven), ACES tone mapping, soft real-time shadows, GTAO ambient occlusion on high quality, subtle bloom only on magic, exponential haze. Custom GLSL for the sky (moving clouds, sun, stars, the dragon constellation), stream water, waterfall, portal and wind-blown vegetation.
- **World:** instanced grass blades and Quaternius CC0 plants, flowers, trees and rocks with vertex-shader wind; 3D cumulus clouds; mountains; sunbeams; butterflies; pollen.
- **Quality presets:** `low` / `med` / `high` (auto-picked; overridable in Settings or with `?q=`). A post-processing hook loads an optional `js/pipeline.js` (`createPipeline(...)`) and falls back to the built-in composer.
- **Characters:** a swappable character layer driven by [`characters.json`](characters.json): rigged **GLB** (AnimationMixer with crossfades), **sprite/billboard** (illustrated frames), or the built-in **procedural** fallback. The current GLB characters are placeholders.
- **PWA / Android-ready:** `manifest.json` + `sw.js` (offline app shell, music cached as it plays), relative paths, installable; ready to wrap with Capacitor or a Trusted Web Activity later.
- **Audio:** Web Audio graph (master / music / SFX gains), lazy-loaded 96 kbps MP3 playlist, synthesized SFX.
- **Code map:** `js/main.js` (game, tutorial, UI), `js/world.js` (meadow), `js/characters.js` (character layer), `js/audio.js`, `js/save.js`, `js/cheats.js`, `js/textures.js` (procedural textures).

## Workflow

1. **Creator input.** Jarred / LIL SYNN gives direction (story, characters, features, approvals); the signed master spec and plot are the source of truth.
2. **Plan.** Hark AI breaks each request into stages that follow the spec's development order.
3. **Build.** Hark AI codes the game (with ChatGPT assistance and Claude technical advice); helper workers source CC0 assets and build character/graphics experiments outside the repo.
4. **Test.** Every stage is checked in a real headless Chromium (WebGL): scripted gameplay flow, cheat rules, audio persistence, saves, touch controls, and screenshot reviews.
5. **Ship.** Commit and push to `main` only (no branches, no pull requests). Vercel deploys lilsynn.com automatically.
6. **Track.** This work list is updated with every push.
