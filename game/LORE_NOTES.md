# LIL SYNN - ROAD TO HER: lore notes

## Pink Dragon — PENDING CONFIRMATION (do not treat as canon)
The spec says the Pink Dragon's identity, history and motives must come from the original LIL SYNN **"Rescue You"** video.
- The **video is not in the Drive folder** (audio only: `ALBUMS/HEAL/Rescue You.mp3` and `ACOUSTIC/Rescue You - Acoustic Version.mp3`). No video has been inspected.
- The lyrics include: *"I'll show up and rescue you from your tower … I'll lead you past the dragon to my land."*
- So all we can say today: the song frames a rescue from a tower, past a dragon, toward "my land". **Everything else about the dragon must be confirmed with LIL SYNN (Jarred) before it is written into dialogue or cutscenes.**
- Stage 1 only foreshadows the dragon visually (a faint dragon-shaped constellation in the meadow sky). No dragon backstory is stated in game.

## Shadow Man (plot, 2026-11-28)
Not evil. A mysterious mentor who seems like an enemy at first; he sets challenges that teach courage, strength, kindness and compassion. Stage 1 dialogue keeps him ambiguous ("Not ready yet, little star… but you will be." / "If you want her back, follow the music.").

## Stage 1 foreshadowing (deliberately unexplained)
A Sonic Blast note briefly turns black; a shadowy figure appears between distant trees while the cat stares at it; a flower nearby wilts; a strange, slightly out-of-tune melody plays; a dragon constellation shimmers in the sky; a whale-shaped cloud drifts overhead (the Dreaming Whale); the portal flickers pink.
